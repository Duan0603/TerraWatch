# 3.11 Offline Geofencing — nội dung đã sửa (để copy vào SRS)

Đối chiếu với: `apps/mobile/lib/features/geofence/*` (GeofenceScreen, GeofenceEvaluator, GeofenceMonitor, ForecastRepository, SqliteZoneStore, ForecastZone, device_location), `home/presentation/home_screen.dart`, `main_shell.dart`, và `services/core-api` (ForecastController `GET /api/v1/forecasts/offline-sync`, ForecastRepository.listApprovedUnexpired).

Ảnh: `docs/srs/3.11-screens/fig46-synchronize-forecast-zones.png`, `fig47-evaluate-device-location.png`, `fig48-view-cache-status.png`.

Những chỗ sai chính so với bản cũ:
- Không có "home monitor screen" tự kiểm tra vị trí. Việc kiểm tra nằm ở màn **"Cảnh báo vị trí"**, mở từ thẻ **"Cảnh báo vị trí ngoại tuyến"** trên Home.
- Không có màn riêng **"Trạng thái dữ liệu"**. Số vùng đã lưu và giờ đồng bộ cuối hiện ngay trên màn "Cảnh báo vị trí" và trên thẻ ở Home.
- Không có các thành phần sau: thanh tiến trình đồng bộ, nhãn vị trí hiện tại, nút "Xem vùng trên bản đồ", nút "Tắt còi", huy hiệu độ mới, bộ đếm vùng còn hiệu lực, nhãn giờ hết hạn sớm nhất.
- App **không dùng** `serverTime` để chỉnh giờ (máy chủ có trả về nhưng app dùng giờ của máy).
- Khi mất mạng, app vẫn gọi đồng bộ, lỗi thì giữ bộ đệm cũ. Đồng bộ chạy nền khi mở app hoặc quay lại app, lỗi bị bỏ qua.
- Cảnh báo trên máy **không lưu** vào lịch sử cảnh báo.
- Khi đang có vùng còn hiệu lực, app luôn báo động, kể cả khi lần đồng bộ cuối đã quá 24 giờ.

## 3.11.1 Synchronize Forecast Zones

**Function Trigger:**
- **Primary Trigger:** The mobile application synchronizes the forecast zone cache in the background when it starts and each time it returns to the foreground.
- **Secondary Trigger:** The citizen taps "Đồng bộ (cần mạng)" on the "Cảnh báo vị trí" screen before travelling into an area without coverage.

**Function Description:**
- **Role:** Citizen, System
- **Purpose:** Downloads the complete set of approved and unexpired forecast zones and replaces the local cache in one transaction, so that on-device geofencing works without network connectivity.
- **Interface:** The synchronization part of the "Cảnh báo vị trí" screen includes the following components:
  - **Synchronize Button:** "Đồng bộ (cần mạng)" starts a manual synchronization; it is disabled while a synchronization or a location check is running.
  - **Cached Zone Counter:** Displays "Vùng dự báo đã lưu trên máy: N".
  - **Last Synchronized Label:** Displays "Đồng bộ lần cuối: dd/MM HH:mm", or "Chưa đồng bộ lần nào" when the cache has never been filled.
  - **Synchronization Result Message:** Displays "Đã đồng bộ N vùng dự báo" or the reason why the synchronization failed.
- **Screen Layout:** Figure 46. Synchronize Forecast Zones Screen Layout

**Function Details:**
- **Data:**
  - **Forecast Zone Set:** Response of GET /api/v1/forecasts/offline-sync: the server time and the list of forecasts that are approved, active, available, belong to an active assessment unit and whose valid_until is still in the future. Each zone carries its run identifier, unit identifier and version, hazard level, probability, issue time, validity window, model version and polygon geometry.
  - **Bounding Box:** Minimum and maximum latitude and longitude computed on the device from the polygon and stored with the zone.
  - **Synchronization Time:** Device time (UTC) at which the cache was last replaced, stored in the local cache metadata.
- **Validation and Business Rules:**
  - **In Normal Cases:**
    - **If the synchronization succeeds:** The application deletes the old zones, inserts the new ones and records the synchronization time inside a single SQLite transaction, so that the cache is never left half updated, and displays "Đã đồng bộ N vùng dự báo".
    - **If a zone that was previously cached is absent from the returned set:** It is removed by the replacement, because absence from the set means that the zone has been withdrawn, superseded or expired.
    - **If the returned set is empty:** The application stores an empty cache and records the synchronization time, so that the device knows that it is up to date and that no zone is currently active.
    - **If the synchronization runs in the background:** Any failure is ignored silently and the previous cache is kept.
  - **In Abnormal Cases:**
    - **If the device has no network connectivity or the request fails:** The previous cache is kept with its original synchronization time, so that the data is later reported as stale rather than as current, and the screen displays the connection error.
    - **If the server returns malformed data:** The application displays "Dữ liệu dự báo từ máy chủ không hợp lệ" and keeps the previous cache.
    - **If the local database cannot be written:** The transaction is rolled back, the previous cache remains intact and the screen displays "Không đọc được dữ liệu dự báo trên máy".
    - **If the session token has expired:** The server returns 401 Unauthorized; the application clears the session and returns the citizen to the Sign In screen, and the existing cache is kept.

## 3.11.2 Evaluate Device Location

**Function Trigger:**
- **Primary Trigger:** The citizen taps the "Cảnh báo vị trí ngoại tuyến" card on the Home screen and then taps "Kiểm tra vị trí" on the "Cảnh báo vị trí" screen.
- **Secondary Trigger:** The citizen turns on "Theo dõi khi đang mở màn hình này"; while the screen stays open, the position is evaluated again each time the device moves at least 25 metres.

**Function Description:**
- **Role:** Citizen, System
- **Purpose:** Determines entirely on the device whether the citizen is inside an active forecast zone, and distinguishes clearly between an active warning, stale data and the absence of an active zone.
- **Interface:** The "Cảnh báo vị trí" screen includes the following sections:
  - **Page Title:** Displays "Cảnh báo vị trí".
  - **Status Card:** Displays the evaluation result with an icon, a title and a detail line: "Chưa kiểm tra vị trí", "Bạn đang trong vùng cảnh báo", "Dữ liệu dự báo đã cũ" or "Chưa có cảnh báo hiện hành".
  - **Hazard Level and Validity:** In the warning state, the detail line displays "Mức nguy cơ: … Hiệu lực đến dd/MM HH:mm".
  - **Stale Reason:** In the stale state, the detail line explains why: never synchronized, synchronization older than 24 hours, the forecast for this place has expired, or every cached forecast has expired.
  - **Check Location Button:** "Kiểm tra vị trí" reads the current position and evaluates it.
  - **Watch Switch:** "Theo dõi khi đang mở màn hình này" with the subtitle "Báo động ngay khi bạn bước vào vùng cảnh báo".
  - **Error Message:** Displays why the position could not be obtained or the cache could not be read.
  - **Privacy Note:** States that the position is processed only on the phone and that "chưa có cảnh báo" is not a confirmation of safety.
- **Screen Layout:** Figure 47. Evaluate Device Location Screen Layout

**Function Details:**
- **Data:**
  - **Device Position:** Latitude and longitude from the device GPS; if no fresh fix is available within 30 seconds, the last known position is used when it is at most 10 minutes old. The position is never sent to the server.
  - **Cached Forecast Zones:** Zones stored locally with their bounding box, polygon, hazard level and validity window.
  - **Evaluation Time:** Device time (UTC); a zone is valid when valid_from ≤ now < valid_until.
  - **Synchronization Time:** Time of the last cache replacement, used to judge staleness against the 24-hour limit.
- **Validation and Business Rules:**
  - **In Normal Cases:**
    - **If candidate zones exist:** The application first selects the zones whose bounding box contains the position with an SQLite query, and only then tests polygon containment, so that the evaluation remains fast with large zone sets.
    - **If the position falls inside at least one zone that is valid at the evaluation time:** The application reports "Bạn đang trong vùng cảnh báo", ranks the zones by hazard level (highest first, then earliest expiry) and raises an emergency notification "NGUY HIỂM: BẠN ĐANG Ở VÙNG CẢNH BÁO SẠT LỞ" with a full-screen intent, the siren sound and vibration. The alarm is raised once per zone while the screen is open, not for every GPS point. This warning takes priority over every stale state.
    - **If the position falls inside no valid zone and the cache is fresh:** The application reports "Chưa có cảnh báo hiện hành" and states explicitly that this is not a confirmation of safety.
    - **If the evaluation runs without network connectivity:** It completes entirely on the device, because it uses only the local cache and GPS.
  - **In Abnormal Cases:**
    - **If the cache has never been synchronized:** The application reports "Dữ liệu dự báo đã cũ" with the reason that no forecast has been downloaded yet, and asks the citizen to synchronize when a network is available.
    - **If the last synchronization is older than 24 hours:** The application reports the state as stale, because zones may have been withdrawn or superseded since then.
    - **If the position falls inside a zone whose validity window has passed:** The application reports the state as stale and shows when the forecast expired. An expired forecast is never reported as an all-clear.
    - **If every cached zone has expired:** The application reports that every stored forecast has expired.
    - **If the location permission has been denied or GPS is off:** The application displays "Cần bật GPS và cấp quyền vị trí để kiểm tra vùng cảnh báo" and no evaluation is performed; if watching was on, it is turned off.
    - **If no position can be obtained:** The application displays "Không lấy được vị trí hiện tại. Hãy bật GPS và thử lại".

## 3.11.3 View Cache Status

**Function Trigger:**
- **Primary Trigger:** The citizen opens the Home screen, whose "Cảnh báo vị trí ngoại tuyến" card shows how many forecast zones are stored on the device.
- **Secondary Trigger:** The citizen opens the "Cảnh báo vị trí" screen, which shows the cached zone count and the last synchronization time, for example after a stale result.

**Function Description:**
- **Role:** Citizen
- **Purpose:** Shows whether forecast data is stored on the device and when it was last synchronized, so that the citizen can tell a real absence of warnings from missing information.
- **Interface:** The cache status is shown in the following places:
  - **Offline Warning Card:** Card "Cảnh báo vị trí ngoại tuyến" on the Home screen; tapping it opens the "Cảnh báo vị trí" screen.
  - **Card Status Text:** "Chưa có vùng dự báo trên máy. Bấm để đồng bộ khi có mạng." or "Đã lưu N vùng dự báo trên máy. Bấm để kiểm tra vị trí của bạn."
  - **Cached Zone Counter:** "Vùng dự báo đã lưu trên máy: N" on the "Cảnh báo vị trí" screen.
  - **Last Synchronized Label:** "Đồng bộ lần cuối: dd/MM HH:mm" or "Chưa đồng bộ lần nào".
  - **Stale Status Card:** "Dữ liệu dự báo đã cũ" with the reason, shown after a location check when the cache cannot be relied upon.
- **Screen Layout:** Figure 48. View Cache Status Screen Layout

**Function Details:**
- **Data:**
  - **Cached Zone Count:** Number of rows in the local forecast_zones table.
  - **Synchronization Time:** Time of the last cache replacement stored in the local cache metadata.
  - **Freshness Limit:** Age beyond which the cache is considered stale, fixed at 24 hours.
- **Validation and Business Rules:**
  - **In Normal Cases:**
    - **If zones are stored on the device:** The Home card displays "Đã lưu N vùng dự báo trên máy".
    - **If the citizen returns from the "Cảnh báo vị trí" screen:** The Home card reloads the zone count.
    - **If the citizen synchronizes successfully:** The counter and the last synchronization time are updated immediately.
  - **In Abnormal Cases:**
    - **If no zone is stored on the device:** The Home card displays "Chưa có vùng dự báo trên máy. Bấm để đồng bộ khi có mạng."
    - **If the cache has never been synchronized:** The screen displays "Chưa đồng bộ lần nào", and a location check reports "Dữ liệu dự báo đã cũ" instead of an all-clear.
    - **If the cache is older than 24 hours or every zone has expired:** A location check reports "Dữ liệu dự báo đã cũ" and states explicitly that this is not a confirmation of safety.
    - **If the local database cannot be read:** The Home card shows 0 zones.

## Ghi chú cho nhóm (không đưa vào SRS)
- Theo dõi vị trí chỉ chạy khi màn "Cảnh báo vị trí" đang mở. Chưa có theo dõi nền (background geofencing).
- `serverTime` có trong phản hồi nhưng app chưa dùng để bù lệch giờ của máy.
- Cảnh báo phát trên máy không được lưu vào màn "Lịch sử cảnh báo".
- Endpoint `/api/v1/landslides/offline-sync` và `/geofence` vẫn còn ở backend nhưng app mobile chỉ dùng `/api/v1/forecasts/offline-sync`.
